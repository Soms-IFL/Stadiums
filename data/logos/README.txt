TEAM LOGOS
==========

Drop a logo image in this folder and the site picks it up the next time
someone opens or refreshes the page. No restart needed.

Name the file after the team. Any of these work (capitals, spaces and
dashes don't matter):

  Miskauga Boulders      boulders.png  miskauga.png  MIS.png
  Laiyford Evergreens    evergreens.png  laiyford.png  LAI.png
  Greene Hill Brawlers   brawlers.png  greene-hill.png  GHB.png
  Parikia Poseidons      poseidons.png  parikia.png  PAR.png
  Dennison Spears        spears.png  dennison.png  DEN.png
  Kyleton Polar Bears    polar-bears.png  kyleton.png  KYL.png
  Ashford Smokers        smokers.png  ashford.png  ASH.png
  Argiroupolis Silvers   silvers.png  argiroupolis.png  ARG.png

Full names work too, e.g. "Miskauga Boulders.png".

Tips
- Transparent PNG works best. Square-ish images, around 256x256 or larger.
- .webp, .svg and .jpg also work.
- Teams without a logo keep the coloured badge with their abbreviation.
